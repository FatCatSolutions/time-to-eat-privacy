## Frequently asked questions

### How do meal reminders work?
Set up your daily meal schedule in the app and Time to Eat sends a gentle reminder at each meal. You can snooze a reminder or mark a meal as done. Turn reminders on or off per meal in Settings.

### I'm not getting notifications.
Make sure notifications are allowed for Time to Eat in your device settings, and that the meal's reminder is turned on in the app. On Android, also allow exact alarms and disable battery optimisation so reminders arrive on time.

### What does Premium include?
Premium unlocks a continuous stream of AI recipe ideas, the full recipe list for each meal, and your saved favourites. All meal-reminder features are free.

### How do I restore my subscription?
Open the paywall and tap **Restore Purchase**. Your subscription is tied to your App Store or Google Play account.

### How do I cancel my subscription?
You can cancel anytime from your store account: on **iOS** via Settings &rarr; your name &rarr; Subscriptions, or on **Android** via the Play Store &rarr; Subscriptions. Premium stays active until the end of the current billing period.
